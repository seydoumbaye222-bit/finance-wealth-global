Investing in Carbon Capture and Carbon Credits in 2024: Outlook and Opportunities

In 2024, the fight against global warming is accompanied by strong economic momentum surrounding carbon capture and carbon credits, offering investors a range of opportunities that are both innovative and aligned with ESG requirements.

The Carbon Capture Market: 2024 Overview

The carbon capture (CCS) sector experienced sustained growth last year, driven by pilot projects in Europe and North America as well as government incentives. According to the latest estimates, over 30 facilities are operational, and several megaprojects are in the financing phase, reflecting growing interest from institutional players.

Emerging Technologies and Key Players

Among the most promising technologies are post-combustion capture, pre-combustion capture, and direct air capture (DAC). Companies such as Climeworks, Carbon Engineering, and major oil groups are investing heavily to improve efficiency and reduce costs, thereby creating venture capital and green bond investment opportunities.

Carbon Credits: Mechanisms, Pricing, and Regulation

Carbon credits, originating from both voluntary and compliance markets, represent an essential financial lever. In 2024, voluntary credit prices range between $15 and $30 per ton, while the European Union Emissions Trading System (EU ETS) maintains prices around €80 per ton. New EU rules and Global Climate Forum initiatives are enhancing transparency and traceability, boosting investor confidence.

Investment Strategies and Risks to Consider

Investors can choose between purchasing direct shares in CCS companies, funds specializing in the energy transition, or bonds tied to capture projects. However, they must remain vigilant in the face of regulatory uncertainties, credit price fluctuations, and technological challenges that could affect medium-term profitability.

Conclusion

In summary, 2024 represents a turning point for green finance, where carbon capture and carbon credits offer attractive return prospects while meeting sustainability requirements; market participants who combine due diligence with a long-term vision will be best positioned to benefit from this expanding market.
