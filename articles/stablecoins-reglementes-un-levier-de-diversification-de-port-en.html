Regulated stablecoins: a lever for portfolio diversification in 2024

In 2024, the emergence of a more structured regulatory framework around stablecoins opens new prospects for investors seeking to optimise portfolio diversification while managing the risks associated with the volatility of traditional cryptocurrencies.

Regulatory framework for stablecoins in 2024

U.S., European and Asian financial authorities have issued, during the year, specific guidelines governing the issuance, custody and transparency of stablecoins. Among the flagship measures are the requirement for reserves to be 100% backed by liquid assets, quarterly audits and direct supervision of issuers by regulators, thereby strengthening institutional investors' confidence.

Stability and correlation with traditional assets

Unlike volatile cryptocurrencies, regulated stablecoins offer near-zero volatility, comparable to that of fiat currencies. Correlation studies conducted by audit firms in 2024 show that their correlation with government bonds or large-cap equities remains marginal, making them an effective tool for reducing the overall risk of a diversified portfolio.

Integration of stablecoins into a multi-asset strategy

Wealth managers now incorporate stablecoins as an intermediate asset class, facilitating cross-border transfers, instant market access and daily liquidity. They can serve as a “cash-wrapper” to quickly reallocate funds among equities, bonds, tokenised real estate or commodities, while benefiting from blockchain traceability that complies with KYC/AML requirements.

Residual risks and best management practices

Despite regulation, risks remain: concentration of reserves at a few banks, vulnerability to monetary policy shifts and technological risks linked to smart contracts. Best practices recommend diversification across multiple stablecoin issuers, regular verification of reserve audits, and the use of third-party custodial solutions certified ISO 27001.

Conclusion

In sum, regulated stablecoins now constitute a strategic component for diversifying portfolios, providing instant liquidity and reducing exposure to cryptocurrency market fluctuations, provided they are incorporated with rigor and residual risks are monitored.
