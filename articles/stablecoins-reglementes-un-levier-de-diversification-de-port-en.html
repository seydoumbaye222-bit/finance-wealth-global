Regulated Stablecoins: a Portfolio Diversification Lever in 2024

In 2024, the emergence of a more structured regulatory framework around stablecoins opens new opportunities for investors seeking to optimise portfolio diversification while managing the risks associated with the volatility of traditional cryptocurrencies.

Regulatory Framework for Stablecoins in 2024

U.S., European and Asian financial authorities have issued, throughout the year, specific guidelines governing the issuance, custody and transparency of stablecoins. Among the flagship measures are the requirement for reserves 100% backed by liquid assets, quarterly audits, and direct supervisory oversight of issuers by regulators, thereby strengthening the confidence of institutional participants.

Stability and Correlation with Traditional Assets

Unlike volatile cryptocurrencies, regulated stablecoins deliver near-zero volatility, comparable to fiat currencies. Correlation studies conducted by audit firms in 2024 show that their correlation with government bonds or large-cap equities remains marginal, making them an effective tool for reducing the overall risk of a diversified portfolio.

Integration of Stablecoins into a Multi-Asset Strategy

Wealth managers now incorporate stablecoins as an intermediate asset class, facilitating cross-border transfers, instant market access and daily liquidity. They can serve as a "cash-wrapper" to quickly reallocate funds between equities, bonds, tokenised real estate or commodities, while benefiting from blockchain traceability that complies with KYC/AML requirements.

Residual Risks and Best Management Practices

Despite regulation, risks persist: concentration of reserves in a few banks, vulnerability to monetary-policy shifts, and technological risks linked to smart contracts. Best practices recommend diversifying across multiple stablecoin issuers, regularly verifying reserve audits, and using third-party custodial solutions certified to ISO 27001.

Conclusion

In summary, regulated stablecoins now represent a strategic component for diversifying portfolios, providing instant liquidity and reducing exposure to cryptocurrency market fluctuations, provided they are integrated rigorously and residual risks are monitored.
